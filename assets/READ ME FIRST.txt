KVNG Studio — test build (macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel)

1. Drag "KVNG Studio.app" into your Applications folder.

2. Open it. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app for malware
   (it's a test build that isn't notarized by Apple yet). To allow it:
     • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to KVNG Studio. Confirm with your password.
   You only need to do this once.

3. On first launch:
     • Your audio output, buffer size, MIDI devices and plugin folders are in
       File → Settings… (⌘,).
     • Plugins: Plugins → Find new plugins scans your VST3 and Audio Unit plugins
       (each one is checked in a separate process, so a broken plugin can't crash it).
     • macOS asks for microphone access the first time you record audio.

4. You can open KVNG projects (.kvng) and FL Studio projects (.flp).
   Sample import accepts WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, Ogg/Vorbis, and M4A audio.
   The mixer fills the workspace (F9); use "Detach mixer" to move it to its
   own window, or "Full screen" to use the whole display.

If something goes wrong, note what you did and send a screenshot. Projects are
autosaved every few minutes; after a crash, KVNG Studio offers to recover them.
